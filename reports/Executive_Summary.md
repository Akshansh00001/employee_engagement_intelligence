# Executive Summary
## Employee Engagement & Burnout Intelligence System

### Purpose
Provide decision-makers with an aggregated view of employee engagement, work-life balance, overtime exposure and career-stage patterns so that HR can investigate emerging experience gaps earlier.

### Snapshot from supplied data
- Employees/records: 1,470
- Mean engagement index: 68.08/100
- Mean work-life balance index: 69.03/100
- Overtime share: 28.3%
- High exposure flag: 8.57%
- Attrition rate: 16.12%

### Recommended actions
1. Review teams where overtime and low work-life balance overlap.
2. Investigate satisfaction dimensions separately instead of relying on a single average.
3. Review career progression and role-tenure patterns at an aggregated level.
4. Run confidential employee listening sessions before selecting interventions.
5. Repeat the same measures quarterly to detect changes over time.

### Governance
The exposure flag is not a diagnosis of burnout. Do not use individual-level scores for employment decisions. Report only sufficiently large groups, restrict access to sensitive HR data, and verify the dataset's source before making company-specific claims.

### Decision requested
Approve a pilot for aggregated workforce analytics, confidential follow-up with priority groups, and longitudinal measurement to evaluate whether interventions improve employee experience.
